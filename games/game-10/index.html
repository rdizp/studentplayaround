How to Play Word Search

1. Select Words on Grid

Find any word from the Target Words panel. Click or tap the first letter, then click or tap the last letter. Connect letters horizontally, vertically, or diagonally!

2. Classroom Facilitator Shortcuts

  • T Switch Turn: Pass turn to the other team.
  • H Hint: Highlight the starting letter of a missing word.
  • F Reveal All: Uncover every word on the board.
  • Space Add Point: Manually award a point.
  • R New Grid: Generate a fresh puzzle grid.

3. Timer & Discussion Pause

Use the Pause button on the top timer bar when pausing for student discussions or explanations.
